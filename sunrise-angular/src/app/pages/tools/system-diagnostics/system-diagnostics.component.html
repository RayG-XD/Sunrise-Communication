Frequently Diagnosed Telecom & Security Failures

Common real-world field scenarios observed across housing societies and commercial offices in Thane and Mumbai MMR.

Why Do CCTV Hard Drives Fail So Often?

Surveillance systems write video data 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Standard desktop computer hard drives are not built for continuous thermal wear and fail within 12–18 months. Always specify 24/7 surveillance-rated drives like WD Purple or Seagate SkyHawk with AllFrame firmware.

How Does Monsoon Rain Affect Intercom Lines?

During Mumbai monsoons, moisture condenses inside open building duct risers and floor junction boxes. Unsealed screw terminals short-circuit, causing loud 50Hz electrical hums or completely dead extensions. We solve this permanently using weatherproof Krone DP boxes with silicone gel crimps.

Why Does an Elevator Camera Video Flicker?

Elevator shafts contain high-voltage 415V three-phase motors and variable frequency drives (VFD) that emit severe electromagnetic interference. Standard cables pick up inductive voltage spikes, causing horizontal rolling bars. We deploy steel-armoured shielded elevator Cat6 cables with ground loop isolators.
