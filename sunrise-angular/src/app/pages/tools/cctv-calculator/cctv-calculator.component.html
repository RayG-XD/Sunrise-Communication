CCTV Storage & Hard Drive FAQs

Expert answers regarding surveillance hard drive reliability, retention policies, and smart codecs.

@if(activeFaqIndex() === 1) {

Surveillance-grade HDDs are specifically engineered for 24/7 continuous write workloads (90% write, 10% read) with AllFrame / ImagePerfect firmware that prevents video frame dropping and withstands extreme heat and vibration inside NVR/DVR chassis. Desktop PC hard drives are built for 8 hours/day read-heavy usage and fail prematurely within 6 to 12 months when used in CCTV recorders.

}
@if(activeFaqIndex() === 2) {

H.265+ (Ultra H.265 / Smart Codec) uses predictive background modeling, intelligent noise suppression, and long-term reference frames. Instead of recording static backgrounds repeatedly (like an empty compound or lobby), it only encodes active motion pixels (such as moving persons or vehicles). This reduces required bitrate and disk usage by 50% to 75% without compromising evidentiary video clarity.

}
